An Exhibition · London · 2026
Lauren Super
Unfiltered
Natural beauty and the digital masks we wear.

Lauren Super is a British multidisciplinary artist working across oil painting, hand-carved stone, ceramic, glass and resin installation.

A body of work that moves from luminous botanical oils to immersive sculpture — on masking, consumption, and what a filtered, always-observed culture is doing to childhood.
