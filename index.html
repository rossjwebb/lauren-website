Lauren Super

Contemporary Multidisciplinary Artist

“My work explores the tension between natural beauty and the digital masks we wear in the modern world — a journey through masking and consumption, always searching for substance in a superficial age.”

I · Beginning

Bliss

Oil on canvas

We begin with Bliss, an oil painting inspired by a Cape Town sunset. A dual perspective of clouds rolling over a purple beach — the vibrant light and magpie shimmer that inspires the work.

II

Smother

Ceramic

Smother is a ceramic piece exploring the weight of motherhood and the desire to protect our children from the forbidden fruit of social media — the struggle to keep them safe from the digital rubbish that can feel so suffocating.

III · Botanical Series

Hibiscus & Cosmos

Oils on canvas · Hampstead Garden Suburb

The botanical series uses deep black backgrounds to make the light pop — painted to find a sense of survival and beauty during a dark and difficult time.

IV

The Golden Child

Hand-carved limestone

A hand-carved limestone fish emerging from a golden egg — the golden child, a treasure born into a world of constant exposure.

Interactive 3D — click and drag to rotate

V

Unfiltered

Glass, oil · Spinning sculpture

A spinning glass sculpture where the viewer sees their own reflection through a digital Snapchat mask — an analogy for the goldfish existence of a modern teenager, always on display and always observed.

VI

Leroy

Ceramic life sculpture

A nod to my African heritage — blending a traditional mask aesthetic with a metallic finish that catches the eye and reflects the beauty I remember from home.

VII

Mark

Mixed media · Resin, glass, taxidermied fish

Mark is a commentary on the architects of social media. He sits atop a glass tank of taxidermied fish, fishing for the next generation — a study on the masks worn by those who control the digital feed.

VIII · The Resin Trilogy

Analogy · Dopamine · Euphoria

Resin

A trilogy on consumption. Analogy, filled with lucky charms. Dopamine, packed with toxic hits of medicine and makeup. And Euphoria, representing the fleeting golden peak.

IX

The Camo Series

Oils on canvas · Large scale

Large-scale oils showing how our children become camouflaged and lost within a superficial, high-speed culture.

X

Seed

Hand-carved limestone

Hand-carved from limestone. A mother holding her child, slowly consumed by the fish — a metaphor for how this digital world can swallow the next generation if we aren’t careful.
