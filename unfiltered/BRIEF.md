# BRIEF — Unfiltered / Lauren Super

**Provenance: partially interviewed.** Questions 3, 6, 7 and the signature-move
seed (Q5) were answered by Ross Webb, who commissions and runs the site, in
session. Questions 2 and 4 are taken from Lauren Super's own published
exhibition text (the Sonya Winner flyer) rather than paraphrased. Questions 1
and 8 are self-authored from her existing site and asset library and are marked
as such. The artist herself was not interviewed directly.

---

## The eight answers

**1. Vibe in three to five words, plus references.** *(Self-authored from the
artist's existing site and the exhibition text.)*
Luminous, watchful, tender, complicit.
References: a natural history museum case at closing time; the deep black
grounds of a Dutch flower painting; the flat glare of a phone screen in a dark
room.

**2. The scroll journey, section by section.** *(Lauren Super's own words, from
the exhibition text.)*
"It begins in the garden. Super's botanical paintings: Cape Town sunsets,
hibiscus and cosmos set against deep black grounds, made as survival and
mindfulness during a difficult time, and stand for a natural beauty the modern
world is losing. From there the work darkens and complicates: masks drawn from
her African heritage; a teenager's 'goldfish existence' seen through a Snapchat
filter; a mother slowly consumed by the fish; the architects of the feed,
fishing for the next generation."

**3. The energy curve.** *(Ross.)*
Calm, then intense, then resolve. Opens serene in the garden, darkens and peaks
at the tank, then settles.

**4. How should someone feel, stage by stage, and the ONE moment they remember?**
*(Curve below, derived from the artist's stated arc in Q2. The peak is the
teenager in the tank: the work the show is named after.)*

**5. One thing this site should do that no site they have seen does.** *(Ross.)*
"The strawberry Snapchat mask tracks your cursor across the page and snaps onto
every face it passes, including, eventually, yours."

**6. How far from premium-minimal.** *(Ross.)*
Editorial. Quiet ground, big type used sparingly, the work carries the colour,
museum-label copy.

**7. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes?** *(Ross.)*
Distinct scenes. Garden, masks, the tank, the feed, with hard cuts between them.

**8. Assets already owned.** *(Self-authored inventory.)*
35 photographs of finished work and three 720p turntable videos of the resin
fish (Analogy, Dopamine, Euphoria), all already in the site repository. No
generated imagery, no API spend. Four of the works contain a human face, which
is what makes the signature move possible: Cosmos (a girl behind a white
flower), Leroy (a carved head), Unfiltered (the teenager in the glass tank),
and Camo (a profile hidden in the sweets).

## The feeling curve

Written before the acts existed. One line per act: the emotion, then the thing
on screen that causes it.

```
0  Composure     paper, her name, the show's dates. Nothing moves yet
1  Wonder        the garden arrives on black, one painting at a time, unhurried
2  Unease        carved faces travel past sideways, labelled like specimens
   · silence ·   the ground goes black and holds one line, alone
3  Implication   the filter you have been dragging lands on the girl in the tank
4  Chill         a resin fish turns under your hand, evenly, like evidence
5  Resolve       paper again, the dates, one line of running text to reply to
```

No two adjacent acts share a feeling. Act 2 is quieter than act 3 by design, so
the peak has something to arrive from.

## The peak

Act 3, The Tank. The largest span on the page by a full viewport-height.

> "the strawberry filter I'd been dragging around the page snapped onto the
> girl's face in the tank, and then it asked whose face it lands on next"

It gets the asset budget (the three angles of the namesake work), the silence
in front of it (the black hold at the end of Act 2), and the most scroll room.

## The tell-someone sentence

> It's the site where a Snapchat filter follows your cursor down the page,
> sticks to every face it passes, and finishes by landing on yours.

## Authored silence

**Deliberate, not dead scroll.** The final ~0.6 viewport-heights of Act 2 is a
black ground carrying one short line and nothing else, immediately before the
peak. If the harness reports low activity there, that is the intended silence
from feel.md §5, not a failure.

## What the visitor must believe by the end

That the filtered, always-observed culture the work describes is one they are
already inside, not one they are looking at.

## What the visitor does next

One action, one label everywhere on the page: **Reply to the invitation.**
Set as a line of running text, per the chaptered-editorial close, never a button
island.
